FAQ

Glass entrances in New Jersey.

What glass entrance systems do you install?+

Frameless glass entrance doors, all-glass pivot systems, automatic sliding glass entry doors, and aluminum-framed commercial entry systems throughout Union, NJ. Each installation is ADA-compliant and custom-fit to your opening dimensions.

How secure are commercial glass entrance doors?+

They use tempered or laminated safety glass paired with heavy-duty commercial-grade panic hardware and deadbolt locks. Laminated glass resists forced entry and holds together under impact — ideal for high-security retail and office entrances.

Are your installations ADA-compliant?+

Yes — all our commercial glass entrances meet ADA accessibility standards, including proper door clearance, low-threshold transitions and automatic opener compatibility. We can also retrofit existing entrances into compliance.

Can you replace a broken entrance door quickly?+

Yes. We offer emergency glass entrance door replacement across all of NJ, with most same-day or next-day board-up and replacement available for vandalism, accident or storm damage. Call us 24/7 for urgent service.

What glass finishes are available?+

Clear tempered, frosted or sandblasted, tinted panels, and Low-E coated glass. Hardware finishes include brushed stainless, matte black, dark bronze and satin chrome — coordinated to your building's aesthetic.

How much does installation cost?+

Cost varies by door size, glass type and hardware. Standard commercial glass entrance installations are competitively priced, and we provide free on-site estimates in Union and surrounding counties — no obligation.
