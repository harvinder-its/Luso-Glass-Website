Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

At Luso Glass, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when visiting our website or contacting our team.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address
  • Project details
  • Any information you submit through contact forms or estimate requests

We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and pages visited to improve website performance.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and estimate requests
  • Schedule appointments
  • Provide residential and commercial glass services
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Send important service updates
  • Meet legal and business obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve functionality, remember preferences, measure website traffic, and understand how visitors use our website. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Information Sharing

We only share information when necessary to:

  • Complete requested services
  • Work with trusted service providers
  • Meet legal requirements
  • Protect our business and customers

Any third parties we work with are expected to protect your information.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect your personal information. While no online system can guarantee absolute security, we continually work to keep your information secure.

Third Party Services

Our website may include links to third party websites, maps, reviews, analytics tools, or social media platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Ask questions about how your information is used

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the revised update date.
