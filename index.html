Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How much does glass repair or replacement cost in Newark, NJ?

Cost depends on the type of glass, the size of the piece, and how complex the installation is. A small mirror costs far less than a custom frameless shower enclosure or a commercial storefront system. We provide free on-site estimates with an itemized quote broken out by glass, hardware, and labor, so you know exactly what you're paying for before any work starts. Most Newark-area estimates are scheduled within 1–2 business days of your call.

Are your glass estimates really free, with no obligation?

Yes, every estimate is completely free and comes with no obligation to hire us. We visit your property in person, take exact measurements, and walk you through your material and pricing options face to face rather than quoting sight unseen over the phone. Most Newark-area visits are scheduled within 1–2 business days of your call, and you'll leave with a real, itemized quote, not a vague ballpark.

Is Luso Glass licensed and insured in New Jersey?

Yes. Luso Glass is fully licensed and insured to perform residential and commercial glass work throughout New Jersey. As a family-owned company operating in Newark since 1993, every project, from a single mirror to a full commercial storefront, is completed by our own in-house team and covered under our insurance rather than handled by subcontractors.

How fast can you respond to an emergency glass repair in Newark?

For broken storefront glass, shattered doors, or a smashed window, we treat it as urgent. Most Newark-area emergency calls get a technician on-site within 1–2 hours, with response times running 2–4 hours further out in the counties we serve. If the glass cannot be replaced immediately, we secure the opening with a temporary board-up until permanent replacement is scheduled.

Can you fabricate glass for older homes or non-standard window and door sizes?

Yes, this is common work for us, especially in older homes throughout Newark and the surrounding counties where original openings were rarely built to today's standard sizes. We measure every job on-site and fabricate the glass to that exact dimension instead of forcing a stock size to fit. This applies to everything from vintage window panes to custom shower enclosures in older bathrooms.

What's the difference between tempered and laminated glass, and which one do I need?

Tempered glass is heat-treated to be several times stronger than standard glass and breaks into small, blunt pieces if it shatters, which is why it's the standard choice for shower doors, railings, and storefronts. Laminated glass has a plastic interlayer bonded between two panes, so it stays intact even when cracked, making it a common choice for security-focused entrances and certain commercial applications. We fabricate both in-house and can recommend the right option once we see your specific project.
