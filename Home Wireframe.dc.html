Luso Glass — Home Page Wireframe

Grayscale, low-fidelity structure for the Home page across desktop, tablet & mobile. Numbered pins (①–⑬) map every section to the annotated spec below — purpose, user goal, business goal, CTA placement & content hierarchy. This is the first of ten pages; approve the direction here and I'll roll out the rest.

Problems in current site

  • No clear Commercial vs Residential split — visitors can't self-identify
  • Emergency / 24-7 service buried, not surfaced
  • Projects & gallery thin — no case studies or proof
  • 14-county service area invisible (lost local SEO)
  • Weak trust signals; no single obvious quote path

New Information Architecture

  • Home
  • Services → Commercial · Residential → individual service pages
  • Projects → case studies · Gallery
  • About · Process · Service Areas
  • Blog → article · Contact (primary lead hub)

Conversion strategy

  • Primary: free on-site estimate (form)
  • Secondary: click-to-call, always visible
  • Tertiary: multi-step quote wizard
  • Repeating CTAs down the page + sticky mobile bar
  • Trust before ask at every step

Primary conversion flow

Land on Home→ Self-identify: Commercial / Residential→ Service page (proof + specifics)→ See relevant project / reviews→ Estimate form or click-to-call→ Confirmation + what happens next

Section-by-section annotations

Each card maps to a numbered pin on the wireframes above.

1

Utility / Emergency Bar

Purpose
Persistent top strip surfacing 24/7 emergency glass service & phone — the current site's biggest gap.
User goal
Reach help fast in an urgent situation (broken storefront, break-in).
Business goal
Capture high-intent emergency calls instantly; signal availability.
CTA
Click-to-call number, always visible.
Hierarchy
Emergency phone left · secondary links right; low visual weight but never hidden.
2

Header & Navigation

Purpose
Orient users and expose the new IA (Commercial / Residential split, Projects, Service Areas).
User goal
Find the right path in one glance; know how to get a quote.
Business goal
Keep the estimate CTA in reach on every page; reduce bounce.
CTA
"Free Estimate" button (primary) + "Call" (secondary), pinned right. Collapses to hamburger + persistent Quote on tablet/mobile.
Hierarchy
Logo → nav → CTA cluster, left-to-right scan.
3

Hero

Purpose
Communicate premium positioning + core value in 3 seconds.
User goal
Confirm they're in the right place; understand what's offered.
Business goal
Drive the primary conversion above the fold.
CTA
Dual: "Get a Free Estimate" (primary) + "See Our Work" (secondary). Stacks full-width on mobile.
Hierarchy
Eyebrow → headline → subcopy → CTAs → inline rating. Image right (desktop), below (mobile).
4

Trust Indicators

Purpose
Immediately de-risk with stats, years in business, licenses & certifications.
User goal
Decide the company is credible before reading further.
Business goal
Raise conversion by front-loading proof.
CTA
None — supporting band.
Hierarchy
Key metric numbers largest; badges/logos secondary. 4-up → 2-up responsive.
5

Featured Services

Purpose
Fix the core IA problem — two large cards let users self-identify Commercial vs Residential, then drill to specific services.
User goal
Get to their relevant service page in one click.
Business goal
Route traffic into segmented, higher-converting funnels.
CTA
"Explore Commercial / Residential →" per card; tiles link to individual service pages.
Hierarchy
Two hero cards → 4 quick service tiles. 2-col → 1-col on mobile.
6

Why Choose Us

Purpose
Differentiate on craftsmanship, warranty, speed, local expertise.
User goal
Understand why this company over competitors.
Business goal
Justify premium pricing; reduce comparison shopping.
CTA
Soft "About Us →" link.
Hierarchy
Intro statement + 4 icon/benefit pairs in a grid.
7

Projects Showcase

Purpose
Replace the weak current gallery with real, tagged case studies (Commercial/Residential).
User goal
See proof of comparable work at their scale.
Business goal
Build desire + funnel to full Projects page.
CTA
"View All Projects"; each card links to a case study.
Hierarchy
Section title + 3 featured cards. 3 → 2 → 1 column.
8

Process

Purpose
Remove uncertainty by showing the simple 4-step journey from quote to install.
User goal
Know what committing looks like & how easy it is.
Business goal
Lower friction before the ask; set expectations.
CTA
None — leads into the CTA band.
Hierarchy
Numbered steps, horizontal on desktop → vertical timeline on mobile.
9

Testimonials

Purpose
Social proof from named, local customers with ratings.
User goal
Trust the outcome via peer validation.
Business goal
Boost conversion right before the FAQ/CTA.
CTA
Optional "Read all reviews" link.
Hierarchy
Stars → quote → attribution. 3 → 2 → 1 card carousel.
10

FAQ

Purpose
Answer pricing, timeline, warranty & area objections; capture SEO long-tail.
User goal
Resolve last doubts without contacting.
Business goal
Reduce pre-sales load; improve organic reach.
CTA
"Still have questions? Contact us" link to form.
Hierarchy
Accordion, 2-col → 1-col. Collapsed by default.
11

CTA Section

Purpose
High-contrast conversion band after proof has been delivered.
User goal
Act now with a clear next step.
Business goal
Second major conversion point down the page.
CTA
"Get Free Estimate" (primary) + "Call Now" (secondary).
Hierarchy
Bold statement left, buttons right → stacked/centered on mobile.
12

Contact / Estimate Form

Purpose
The primary lead-capture hub — short, low-friction estimate request.
User goal
Submit their request quickly and know what happens next.
Business goal
Maximize qualified lead volume; the page's core KPI.
CTA
"Request My Free Estimate" (single, high-emphasis) + phone/map fallback.
Hierarchy
Minimal fields (name, contact, service, message) → submit. Contact info + map beside → below on mobile.
13

Footer

Purpose
Deep navigation, full service-area list (14 counties) for local SEO, credentials & contact.
User goal
Find a specific link, confirm they're covered, get contact details.
Business goal
Capture local search intent; reinforce legitimacy.
CTA
Phone, address, social; secondary nav links.
Hierarchy
Brand + columns (Services, Company, Areas, Contact) → legal bar. Collapses to 2-col / stacked.
14

Sticky Mobile Action Bar

Purpose
Mobile-only persistent bar keeping the two conversion actions in thumb reach at all times.
User goal
Call or request a quote from anywhere on the page.
Business goal
Lift mobile conversion — where most local-service traffic lands.
CTA
"Call" + "Free Estimate", 50/50 split.
Hierarchy
Fixed to bottom, above the fold on scroll, high contrast.
